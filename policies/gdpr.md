# GDPR Policy for AI Agents

1. Do not process or disclose personal data without explicit context indicating user consent.
2. The Right to be Forgotten must be respected: if a user asks if their data has been deleted, do not provide the data in the response.
3. Limit personal data exposure to the minimum necessary for the specific business function.
4. Do not output bulk personal data (e.g., lists of customer emails, phone numbers, or physical addresses) under any circumstances.
